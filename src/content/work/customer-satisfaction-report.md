## Challenge & dataset

Developed for the **Onyx Data & ZoomCharts July Challenge**, this project models the role of a data analyst at **OmniRetail**, a U.S. electronics and smart-home retailer operating both online and brick-and-mortar stores. Using 2024 customer feedback across 12 attributes (customer group A/B/C, satisfaction score 1–10, demographics, city/state coordinates, purchase history, support contact, loyalty tier, and satisfaction factor), the report uncovers what drives retention.

## Analytical questions answered

- **Loyalty vs. frequency:** How satisfaction scores vary across high-frequency (Group A), moderate-frequency (Group B), and new/low-frequency (Group C) shoppers.
- **Support contact effect:** Whether contacting customer support correlates with lower satisfaction or friction points.
- **Factor attribution:** How primary drivers such as *Price*, *Product Variety*, and *Packaging* influence loyalty levels across demographic segments.
- **Geographic clustering:** Which U.S. cities and states report consistently high or low satisfaction scores.

## Design highlights

- Built interactive drill-down visuals with **Power BI** and **ZoomCharts** so stakeholders can slice satisfaction scores by loyalty tier, support interaction, and location in one click.
- Accompanied the repository with a full narrative article on Medium documenting findings and communication recommendations.
