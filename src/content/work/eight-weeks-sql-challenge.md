## Scope & coverage

Across the **8 Weeks SQL Challenge** and the **Parch and Posey** relational database case study, I solved and documented complex analytical problems entirely in **PostgreSQL**:

- **Data cleaning & normalization:** Fixing malformed timestamps, string nulls, and inconsistent delivery metrics (*Pizza Runner*).
- **Customer retention & SaaS cohorts:** Trial-to-paid conversion, upgrade velocity, and churn tracking (*Foodie-Fi*).
- **Digital funnel attribution:** Web clickstream session modeling for view-to-cart and cart-to-purchase drop-offs (*Clique Bait*).
- **Rolling balances & market baskets:** Running monthly balances (*Data Bank*), pre/post sustainability packaging impact (*Data Mart*), and product-pair combinations (*Balanced Tree*).
- **Relational sales analytics:** Multi-region account and order analysis across the *Parch and Posey* schema.
