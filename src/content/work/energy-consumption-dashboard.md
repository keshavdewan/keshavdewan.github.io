## Problem & approach

Managing multi-utility consumption across commercial buildings requires consolidating water, electricity, and gas telemetry into a single operational view. Rather than cramming every metric onto one crowded canvas, I structured this **Power BI** report into four focused pages: **Overview**, **Water**, **Electricity**, and **Gas**.

## Key findings

- **Water dominance & cost stability:** Water represented **88.49%** of total utility consumption, peaking in 2017 while total cost remained relatively steady — highlighting high-leverage conservation targets.
- **Downward gas trajectory:** Gas usage — the lowest overall category — declined steadily between 2016 and 2019 alongside falling gas expenditure.
- **Actionable drill-downs:** Custom DAX measures compare each utility against remaining total units consumed, allowing facility managers to isolate outlier buildings by city and month.

## Recommendations delivered

- Prioritize targeted water conservation programs in peak-usage buildings.
- Enrich future telemetry with building type and time-of-day granularity for sharper anomaly detection.
