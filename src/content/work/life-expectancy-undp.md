## Overview

Life expectancy at birth is a core indicator of healthcare access, socio-economic resilience, and human development. Working with UNDP data spanning **1990 to 2021**, this project pairs Python exploratory analysis with an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Key questions explored

- How global and regional life expectancy evolved over three decades across hemispheres and UNDP development groups.
- How **HDI Rank (2021)** correlates with gains or plateaus in life expectancy.
- Which regions exhibit the widest intra-regional dispersion.
